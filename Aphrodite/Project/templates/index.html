Cosmetics Supply Chain Optimization

Welcome to our intelligent platform for predictive and prescriptive analysis in the cosmetics supply chain.

🎯 Project Objective

Our goal is to optimize inventory management, forecast production needs, and improve decision-making processes for cosmetic products using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning models.

🚀 Key Features

  • ✅ Stock Clustering & Classification
  • ✅ Sales and Revenue Prediction
  • ✅ Supply Chain Demand Forecasting
  • ✅ Cosmetic Product Recommendation
  • ✅ Quality Detection via Image Analysis

🛠️ Integrated AI Models

  • 📈 Linear and ARIMA Time Series Models
  • 🧠 Deep Learning for Cosmetic Image Classification
  • 🎯 Recommendation Systems based on Product Similarities
  • 🔍 Clustering & Regression with XGBoost and Scikit-Learn

⚙️ Technologies Used

  • 💻 Flask, Bootstrap 5
  • 🧠 Scikit-Learn, TensorFlow, XGBoost, Statsmodels
  • 🗄️ Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib

Empowering smarter supply chain decisions through Artificial Intelligence.
